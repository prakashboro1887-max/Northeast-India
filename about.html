About NE Academic Hub

NE Academic Hub is an open-access educational gateway designed to unify academic notices, institutional repository links, competitive examination syllabi, and recruitment updates across Northeast India[cite: 15].

Our Mission

Students, researchers, and job aspirants in the Northeastern states often face fragmented information scattered across dozens of individual institutional websites[cite: 15]. Our goal is to aggregate verified public announcements—from Gauhati University, SEBA, AHSEC, NEHU, and state recruitment bodies—into a fast, mobile-friendly directory[cite: 15].

What We Curate

  • Career & Apprenticeships: Library, technical, and government recruitment notices[cite: 15].
  • Institutional Study Materials: Open-access e-books, metadata standards, and curriculum guides[cite: 15].
  • Previous Year Question Papers (PYQ): Past board and competitive exam papers for student preparation[cite: 15].
  • Academic Calls for Papers: Conferences, UGC-CARE indexing dates, and scholarly publications[cite: 15].

Open-Access Commitment

We do not charge subscriptions or lock public government gazette notifications behind paywalls. Every syllabus, past question paper, and admission notice remains openly accessible for all candidates.
