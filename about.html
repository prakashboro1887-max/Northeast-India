About NE Academic Hub

NE Academic Hub is an open-access educational gateway designed to unify academic notices, institutional repository links, competitive examination syllabi, and recruitment updates across Northeast India.

Our Mission

Students, researchers, and job aspirants in the Northeastern states often face fragmented information scattered across dozens of individual institutional websites. Our goal is to aggregate verified public announcements—from Gauhati University, SEBA, AHSEC, NEHU, and state recruitment bodies—into a fast, mobile-friendly directory.

What We Curate

  • Career & Apprenticeships: Library, technical, and government recruitment notices.
  • Institutional Study Materials: Open-access e-books, metadata standards, and curriculum guides.
  • Previous Year Question Papers (PYQ): Past board and competitive exam papers for student preparation.
  • Academic Calls for Papers: Conferences, UGC-CARE indexing dates, and scholarly publications.
