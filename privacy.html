Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 2026

At NE Academic Hub, accessible from our GitHub Pages portal, the privacy of our visitors is of paramount importance. This Privacy Policy document outlines the types of information collected and how it is utilized.

Information We Do Not Collect

We do not run user registrations, require logins, or collect personal identifying information (such as bank details, personal telephone numbers, or confidential identity documents).

Log Files & Browser Data

Like most web servers, GitHub Pages may automatically log anonymous standard traffic data, such as internet protocol (IP) addresses, browser types, Internet Service Providers (ISPs), date/time stamps, and referring pages. This data is not linked to personally identifiable information.

Cookies & Local Storage

Our portal utilizes browser local storage solely to retain your UI preferences (such as light or dark theme selections) and Service Workers to enable rapid offline caching of educational pages. We do not store tracking cookies for behavioral surveillance.

Third-Party Links & External Platforms

Our portal references external links to educational boards, universities, and public repositories. We encourage you to review the privacy documentation of any external sites you visit through our directory.
